How to Play

Aim & fire (auto-aim, default): move the mouse where you want the arrow to land and click — the archer looses a shot that arcs right to that spot. Watch the trajectory dots and fire again when the power bar refills.

Aim & fire (classic drag): switch shooting style in Settings, then press and hold the mouse anywhere, drag away from your target to draw the bow, and release to loose. The farther you drag, the farther the arrow flies.

Headshots deal massive damage. Shield-bearers block arrows from the front — wait for openings or use brute force. Captains wear armor that soaks up damage.

Protect the castle. Enemies that reach the wall will hack at it. If the castle falls, the level is lost. Arrows can also be fired straight into the stone.

Destroy the enemy castle. The enemy host pours out of the dark keep on the right — you must bring their castle down to win the level. Aim at it to chip its health, lodge arrows in the walls, and finish it with a blast arrow. Its health bar sits in the HUD and above the castle itself.

Keys: 1–9 · 0 · - · = · [ · ] switch arrows (all 18) · P pause · Esc pause

Progress: earn gold, buy better arrows in the Armory, and unlock the next level with every victory. Stars are awarded for how much castle health remains. Revisit any unlocked level from the Campaign Map to farm extra gold.
